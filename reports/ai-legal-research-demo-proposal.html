Executive Summary

Legal research consumes 30–40% of associate hours. This pilot deploys a domain-tuned AI assistant on your firm's case archive plus public legal corpora (citations, statutes, regulations) to deliver cited answers in seconds, with full source traceability.

Problem & Opportunity

Pain points

  • Manual Boolean search across 5+ databases
  • Junior time billed below realization rate
  • Inconsistent memo quality across teams
  • Citation accuracy risk under deadline pressure

Opportunity

  • Cut research time per matter 40–60%
  • Reclaim ~12 billable hrs/associate/week

  • Built-in citation verification + hallucination guardrails

Proposed Solution

Retrieval-augmented (RAG) legal copilot with three surfaces:

  • Ask — natural-language Q&A grounded in matter archive + statutory corpus, every sentence carries verifiable citation.
  • Draft — generates memo skeletons, motion outlines, discovery summaries in firm style.
  • Compare — side-by-side analysis of opposing case law and jurisdictional splits.

Stack: Claude Opus 4.7 for reasoning, embedding index over your DMS (iManage / NetDocs connectors), strict citation-verification layer rejects unsupported claims before display.
