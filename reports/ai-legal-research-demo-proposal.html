AI Legal Research Demo

Prepared by: Surya AI Date: 2026-05-15 Version: 1.0 Status: Draft

1. Executive Summary

Surya AI proposes a 4-week proof-of-concept demonstrating an AI-powered legal research assistant. System ingests case law, statutes, and internal memos, then answers natural-language questions with cited authority, jurisdiction filters, and confidence scoring. Goal: cut associate research time by 60–75% on common motion-prep tasks.

2. Problem

  • Associates spend 6–10 hrs/week on routine case lookup.
  • Westlaw/Lexis bills scale linearly with usage — no leverage.
  • Internal memo bank not searchable; institutional knowledge stranded.
  • Hallucinated citations from generic LLMs unusable in filings.

3. Proposed Solution

Retrieval Layer

Hybrid BM25 + dense vector search over case corpus, statutes, and firm memos.

Citation Verifier

Every cited case re-fetched and quote-matched before output. Zero hallucinated cites.

Jurisdiction Filter

Scope queries by state/federal circuit. Surface controlling authority first.

Memo Drafter

One-click export to IRAC-format memo with footnoted citations.

4. Technical Approach

Stack

  • LLM Claude Opus 4.7 for reasoning + drafting
  • Embeddings Voyage-3-law for legal-tuned retrieval
  • Vector DB AgentDB w/ HNSW index
  • Frontend Next.js App Router on Vercel
  • Auth Clerk (SSO ready)

Data sources for demo

  • CourtListener bulk dump (federal + select state).
  • Sample firm memo corpus (10–50 anonymized docs).
  • U.S. Code titles relevant to chosen practice area.

8. Risks & Mitigations

  • Hallucinated cites → verifier blocks unverified output.
  • Privileged data leakage → on-prem or VPC-isolated deploy option.
  • Stale case law → nightly delta ingest from CourtListener.
  • Adoption friction → 1-hr training + Slack support channel.
