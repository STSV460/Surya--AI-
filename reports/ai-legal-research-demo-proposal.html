1. Executive Summary

Junior associates spend 15–20 hours per week on case-law research, citation checks, and memo drafting. This proposal outlines a 4-week demo of an AI legal-research assistant that ingests a firm-curated corpus (statutes, judgments, internal memos), answers natural-language questions with grounded citations, and drafts research memos in the firm's house style.

Target outcome: cut first-draft research time by 60% while keeping a verifiable citation trail for every claim.

2. Problem

  • Manual Westlaw / SCC Online queries are slow and surface noisy results.
  • Generic LLMs hallucinate citations — unusable for filings.
  • Internal precedent memos sit in shared drives, undiscoverable at query time.
  • Partners have no fast way to audit how a junior reached a legal conclusion.

3. Proposed Solution

Grounded Q&A

Every answer cites paragraph-level sources from the firm's corpus. No source, no claim.

Memo Drafting

Generate IRAC-structured memos with editable citation footnotes.

Citator Check

Flag overruled, distinguished, or doubted judgments before they ship.

Audit Trail

Replayable session log of every retrieved chunk and reasoning step.

5. Demo Scope

In scope 500-document corpus, 3 practice areas (commercial, IP, employment), single-tenant deployment, partner + 5 associate seats.

Out of scope Multi-jurisdiction support, e-filing integration, billing automation, mobile app.

7. Success Metrics

  • ≥ 90% citation accuracy on 100-question gold-set (manually reviewed).
  • ≥ 60% reduction in median time-to-first-draft memo.
  • Zero hallucinated case names across UAT (hard gate).
  • NPS ≥ 40 from associate cohort.
