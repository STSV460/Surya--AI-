AI-powered legal research, grounded in the citations your firm trusts

A 30-day demo that puts a domain-tuned research agent in front of a partner-led pilot team. Built on retrieval over a curated case-law corpus, statute graph, and the firm's own memos — every answer cites primary sources, every draft is reviewable, nothing leaves the tenant.

Why now

Associate hours bleed on lookup

Avg. 6–9 hrs per matter spent on case discovery and shepherding citations — work that scales linearly with headcount today.

Generic LLMs hallucinate cites

Off-the-shelf chatbots fabricate citations roughly 1 in 6 responses. Unusable in filings without retrieval grounding.

Grounded agents are now viable

Retrieval + tool-use + citation verification close the trust gap. Same stack already shipping in adjacent verticals.

What the demo does

1. Issue-spotted research memo

Paste fact pattern → agent returns a structured memo: issues, controlling authority, persuasive auth, counter-arguments. Every claim links to a verified citation.

2. Citation verifier

Upload a draft brief; agent flags miscites, overruled cases, stale Shepard signals, and pin-cite mismatches.

3. Statute & reg cross-walk

Compare statute versions across jurisdictions or effective dates; surface regulatory commentary tied to each section.

4. Firm-knowledge recall

Search prior memos, deal precedents, and partner notes inside the same tenant — no co-mingling with other firms.
