AI Legal Research Demo

Prepared forProspective Client Date15 May 2026 DocSAI-PROP-2026-014

A two-week pilot demonstrating an AI assistant that researches case law, drafts memos, and surfaces cited precedent — built on Surya AI's retrieval stack and tuned for your jurisdiction.

1. Executive Summary

Associates spend a median of 6.4 hours per matter on first-pass legal research. We propose a contained pilot of Surya Counsel, a retrieval-augmented assistant that ingests your matter brief and returns a cited memorandum in minutes — with every claim linked to source paragraph in the originating case.

The demo runs on a sandboxed corpus of public Indian case law (Supreme Court + High Courts, 1950–2026) and produces output a junior associate can verify, not replace.

2. Problem

  • Manual research scales linearly with billable hours, not headcount.
  • Generic LLMs hallucinate citations — unacceptable in court filings.
  • Existing legal databases (Manupatra, SCC Online) require keyword expertise, not natural-language queries.

3. Proposed Solution

Grounded Retrieval

Every answer cites paragraph-level sources. No citation, no claim.

Jurisdiction-Aware

Filters by court, bench strength, and date — overruled judgments flagged.

Memo Drafting

Outputs facts → issues → analysis → conclusion structure, editable in-browser.

Audit Trail

Every query, source touched, and output exported as signed PDF for compliance.

4. Demo Scope

In Scope

  • Single matter brief input (≤ 5 pages)
  • Research over public Indian SC + Delhi HC judgments
  • Cited memo output in IRAC format
  • One follow-up refinement round per query
  • Web interface, hosted on Surya AI infrastructure

Out of Scope (Phase 1)

  • Private case files or firm-internal precedent
  • Statutory drafting or contract review
  • Multi-user collaboration / role-based access
